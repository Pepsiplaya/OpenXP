## Class chapter routing

The Mage's Wand chapter starts with a Stormwind waypoint for Alliance and an Orgrimmar waypoint for Horde. Quest 1947 also accepts the other faction-appropriate trainers listed in the guide. After Thousand Needles, finish Arathi and the Library on one trip before handing the two quests to Tabetha. Use the outdoor Library approach at Tirisfal 82.60, 33.80. The pinned database records the book object inside area 796 without an indoor map point. Location instructions inside the Library come from the book's room description and player reports. [Scarlet Monastery Library](https://www.icy-veins.com/wow-forever/scarlet-monastery-library-guide)

The Horde Warrior chapter starts with Sorek's Orgrimmar waypoint and lists Torm and Baltus as alternate pickups. Fray Island is area 720 on the Barrens map. The chapter leads along Merchant Coast and waits for the Fray Island subzone before activating the arena pins. The shallow crossing is supported by firsthand observation in Forever. **UNVERIFIED** for the fork's exact travel time and fatigue. [Forever player account](https://www.pcgamer.com/games/world-of-warcraft/im-so-glad-im-not-using-a-questing-addon-in-world-of-warcraft-forever/)

The Tempest's Weapons Horde chapter begins after Elemental Aid (79360). Storm, Earth, and Fire (79361) tracks Water at index 1, Earth at 2, Fire at 3, and Air at 4 in the pinned Forever quest traces. The route collects Air in Desolace and Water on the northern Dustwallow coast before crossing to the Swamp of Sorrows and Badlands. It groups the two Eastern Kingdoms objectives into one trip, then returns to Prate. Drop sources are **VERIFIED BY GAME DATA**. Live drop rates, group credit at the ritual, and comparative travel time are **UNVERIFIED**. [Quest trace](https://github.com/Questie/QuestieDB/blob/e1652ce62113c152da682baf2d6fb85f1396bcfb/src/corrections/Forever/traces/foreverQuestTraces.lua#L359-L418), [Fire](https://db.icy-veins.com/wow-forever/items/274044), [Earth](https://db.icy-veins.com/wow-forever/items/274045), [Water](https://db.icy-veins.com/wow-forever/items/274046), [Air](https://db.icy-veins.com/wow-forever/items/274047)

Verigan's Fist starts after handing The Tome of Valor (1652) to Duthorian Rall. The parent quest (1654) tracks lumber at index 1, Bailor's refined ore reward at 2, Jordan's hammer at 3, and Thundris's purified gem reward at 4. The chapter travels through Loch Modan, Westfall, and Silverpine before crossing to Darkshore. The hammer's stables location comes from historical player reports, backed by the Forever export's instance object. Use the final quest (1806) after turning in the four materials and wait for Jordan to forge the mace. Dungeon approach, clear time, and live animation checks are pending. [Material quest](https://db.icy-veins.com/wow-forever/quests/1654), [Hammer](https://db.icy-veins.com/wow-forever/items/6895), [Player reports](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/quest=1654/the-test-of-righteousness), [Forge](https://db.icy-veins.com/wow-forever/quests/1806)

The Undead Wolfsbane chain uses Ulric Frostveil's Seeking the Kor Gem (95042) for its purified gem (272448). The pinned export links the Enchanted Silver Ingot (267361) to object 629065 and has no spawn for that object. Forever player reports place it next to Arugal in Shadowfang Keep. A Moon-Kissed Blade (95036) shows four objectives on the client-data page. The merged export lists both Trevan's interaction and his note as separate entries, so record the live objective indices before authoring the steps. Ott's Masterwork (95125) has a forge objective between the two Hillsbrad handoffs.
