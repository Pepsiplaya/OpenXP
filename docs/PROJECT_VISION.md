# OpenXP Ultimate

OpenXP should guide every legal World of Warcraft: Forever character from level 1 to 60. The shared leveling route handles common quests and transport. Class and race branches teach important abilities and cover progression worth the detour. Players can follow the suggested route or select verified zone and dungeon chapters.

Prioritize route correctness, leveling time, safe directions, and recovery when players skip or finish a quest early. Introduce solo, safer solo, PvP, dungeon-assisted, quest-focused, and group recommendations as evidence supports them. Record group formation, danger, and gold costs alongside XP.

Ship research in playable increments. Confirm quest prerequisites, objectives, and map points against Forever data. Replay proposed changes off-client and capture the expected outcome in a test. Measure routes in-game before making speed claims. Keep player observations and the route model in sync after game updates.

Keep the fork free under CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 and retain upstream attribution. Build against external reference data in place. See [NOTICE.md](../NOTICE.md), [Research sources](RESEARCH_SOURCES.md), and [Next actions](NEXT_ACTIONS.md).
