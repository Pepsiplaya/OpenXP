# Alliance Mage leveling in WoW Forever

Use **Mage leveling** in the guide menu or type `/oxp mage` for your current chapter, unlearned trainer ranks, wand options, and travel spells. Use `/oxp where` to find your place after a dungeon or optional class quest.

## Start at level 1

**VERIFIED BY GAME DATA**. Human, Gnome, and High-Order Skyborne are the Alliance races that can create a Mage. All three use the bundled Westfall handoff at the default XP rate. The chapter-link check runs from each starter through Stormwind at 59–60. This is a **SIMULATED** check of guide navigation. Keep your Hearthstone and learn flight paths in the starter chapters. At level 4, train Frostbolt to slow enemies or keep Fireball current if you are going Fire. Conjure Water starts at 4 and Conjure Food at 6. Refill both before a long loop. At 8, Polymorph gives you a way to take a susceptible add out of the fight while you handle the first target. ### Optional early equipment

- **Mirror Lake**, level 10. Pick up the flask from Jennea Cannon in Stormwind, fill it at the waterfall in western Elwynn, and return to choose Ley Staff or Ley Orb. The Forever record includes High-Order Skyborne for the main quest. The introductory Speak with Jennea quest lists Human and Gnome, so the Skyborne pickup still needs a live check. [Forever quest record](https://questing.wiki/wow-forever/quests/1861/), [Flask use](https://db.icy-veins.com/wow-forever/items/7207)
- **Mage-tastic Gizmonitor**, level 10 for Human and Gnome. Choose this alternative when you already need to visit Ironforge or western Dun Morogh. Check the hut north of Gnomeregan for Bink's Toolbox, and watch for leper gnome patrols. Choose Arcane Staff or Arcane Orb when you return to Bink. QuestieDB lists it as an alternative to Mirror Lake. [Quest record](https://db.icy-veins.com/wow-forever/quests/1880), [Observed quest details](https://www.icy-veins.com/wow-forever/mage-class-quests)
- **Manaweave Robe**, level 15 for Human and Gnome. Start at the Mage Tower. Use the Cantation and coffers at the Blue Recluse, then bring six Charged Rift Gems from the Silver Stream Mine and ten Linen Cloth to Wynne Larson. Consider the trip when you already have a reason to visit Loch Modan and the Mage Quarter. [Blue Recluse](https://db.icy-veins.com/wow-forever/quests/1920), [Gathering Materials](https://db.icy-veins.com/wow-forever/quests/1921), [Manaweave Robe](https://db.icy-veins.com/wow-forever/quests/1941)

### Study and conjuration

On a Stormwind trip, hand a library book to Garion Wendell at the Mage Tower and finish Research Access to learn Study. The optional chapter routes the Gnomish Tome at Sentinel Hill. This tome is also listed in the inn at Thelsamar. Comprehend Scroll is available from level 6. The exact minimum level at which Garion offers the follow-up is **UNVERIFIED** in the fork. At 60, take Hydrospawn's essence to Lorekeeper Lydros in the Dire Maul Athenaeum through the **Arcane Refreshment** chapter to learn Conjure Water rank 7. Archivist Galford in Stratholme is a recorded source for the Conjure Food rank 7 tome. [Research Access](https://db.icy-veins.com/wow-forever/quests/97286), [Rumi's book](https://db.icy-veins.com/wow-forever/quests/79093), [Book location observations](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/guide/library-book-locations-and-rewards), [Comprehend Scroll](https://db.icy-veins.com/wow-forever/spells/1296017), [Arcane Refreshment](https://db.icy-veins.com/wow-forever/quests/7463), [Conjure Food tome](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/item=22897/tome-of-conjure-food-vii)

## Combat and recovery

- **Solo Frost.** Improved Frostbolt supports repeated casts on scattered targets. Start at range, slow the target, finish a weak target with your wand if it saves a cast, and drink in a clear spot. Frost Nova unlocks at 10, Blink and Evocation at 20, Counterspell at 24, and Cone of Cold at 26. Keep mana for a root or Blink if you might draw another enemy. [Mage spell records](https://zephras.com/forever/enUS/spell/class/mage/)
- **AoE Frost.** Blizzard starts at 20. Try a small pack of melee enemies on flat ground, with room to kite and a retreat path you have cleared. Improved Blizzard tops out at a 40% slow in Blizzard's class notes. Under the November 13 hotfix, Blizzard and proc damage can break Frost Nova. A camp with casters, roamers, or competing groups can cost more time than nearby questing. [Blizzard class notes](https://news.blizzard.com/en-us/article/24302097/world-of-warcraft-forever-class-deep-dives-mage-and-shaman), [November 13 hotfixes](https://news.blizzard.com/en-us/article/24315281/wow-forever-hotfixes-november-13-2026)
- **Fire and Arcane.** Fire can pack more damage into short fights with Fireball and critical-strike talents. Compare time to kill and kills per drink when the target can reach you before it dies. Arcane Blast is a talent. Build only the stacks you can afford and use the buff on another spell. Frostfire Bolt has level 40, 50, and 60 ranks and benefits from Fire and Frost talents. [Blizzard class notes](https://news.blizzard.com/en-us/article/24302097/world-of-warcraft-forever-class-deep-dives-mage-and-shaman), [Frostfire Bolt ranks](https://zephras.com/forever/enUS/spell/1237313/)
- **Dungeon group.** Agree on Polymorph targets with the tank and wait for grouped pulls before using AoE. Bring conjured water and count party formation, travel, recovery, wipes, and hand-ins in the comparison. On the PvP ruleset, pick loops with clear exits and move on if repeated interruptions cut into XP. The optional tower quests can require waiting for opposing control to change. [Blizzard rulesets](https://worldofwarcraft.blizzard.com/en-us/news/24302070), [Tower quest record](https://metabot.gg/en/wow-forever/quests/establishing-new-outposts-9664)

**UNVERIFIED**. A grind beats a quest circuit when it yields more actual XP over the same elapsed time, including drinks, looting, travel, waiting, and deaths. Test both from a matched level and XP, gear, talents, rest state, and quest access. A short quest hand-in burst can look better per hour while leaving most of the level unfinished.
